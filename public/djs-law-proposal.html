Marketing Terms in Plain English

Clarity First
PPC (Pay-Per-Click) & CPC (Cost-Per-Click) You only pay Google or Facebook when someone actually clicks your ad. If 1,000 people see your hoodie ad and don't click, you pay £0. CPC is the actual price per click (e.g. £0.45).
CPA (Cost-Per-Acquisition) How much ad money you spend to get one paying customer. If you spend £40 on ads and sell 2 books, your CPA is £20 per customer.
ROAS (Return On Ad Spend) How much revenue you make back for every £1 you put in. A 2.5x ROAS means every £1,000 in ad spend brings back £2,500 in sales.
Self-Funding Offer (Book Sales That Pay for the Ads) Selling a £16.99 book to completely pay off your advertising bill, which lets you meet potential clients who then hire you for your main legal services (£500–£2,500+) for free.
Exact Match vs Negative Keywords Exact Match tells Google to only show your ad when someone types the exact phrase (e.g. [settlement agreement advice]). Negative keywords block bad searches (e.g. blocking "free" or "word template").
Meta Advantage+ & Conversion Tracking Facebook/Instagram's smart algorithm tests multiple hoodie designs to find the exact people who buy clothing, while a tracking pixel on your website confirms when sales occur.

Strategic Addendum Why This Model Works

The Strategic Rationale: De-risking Client Acquisition

Why traditional digital book marketing fails in legal services—and why this offer architecture is engineered for maximum conversion:

🎯 1. From Passive Ebook to Urgent Solution

People facing redundancy or a family power of attorney crossroads don't want "reading material"—they want immediate certainty. By bundling high-utility email negotiation templates and checklists, we transform a passive digital download into an active, high-perceived-value survival kit.

Result: Higher perceived value justifies a £24.99 price point and doubles checkout conversions.
🛡️ 2. The 100% Fee Rebate (Zero-Friction Bridge)

Traditional PPC leaves the backend conversion to luck. Offering to credit the full £24.99 purchase against legal instructions makes buying the guide completely risk-free. Where applicable, UK employers commonly provide a standard contribution (£350–£750+) toward independent legal advice on qualifying settlements, meaning eligible clients can achieve a full review with negligible or zero net out-of-pocket expense.

Result: The guide becomes a self-funding lead engine that guarantees high-ticket case inquiries.
🚀 3. Algorithmic Focus (Hero SKU vs. SKU Chaos)

Splitting a modest ad budget across 12 zodiac variants starves Meta's machine learning, driving up ad costs. Directing 100% of top-of-funnel traffic into one proven, high-resonance "hero" hoodie concentrates conversion data, lowers CPMs, and lets buyers customize their sign on-page.

Result: Lower Cost-Per-Click and faster algorithmic optimization on Meta.

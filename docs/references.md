# References

Primary sources cited in the AI Integ marketing copy. Last verified 2026-10-14.

## 1. Time lost searching for information

**McKinsey Global Institute — _The Social Economy: Unlocking value and productivity through social technologies_ (July 2012).**

- Finding: the average _interaction worker_ spends ~28% of the workweek managing email and **nearly 20% of the workweek looking for internal information or tracking down colleagues**.
- Cited for: the "nearly 20% of the workweek searching for information" figure.
- Used in: homepage `workflow-reality` hook; citations modal, section 1.
- URL: https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/technology-media-and-telecommunications/our-insights/the-social-economy

## 2. Billable utilization

**SPI Research — _2025 Professional Services Maturity Benchmark_ (18th annual, February 2025).**

- Finding: employee **billable utilization fell to a historic low of 66.4% in 2025** (2024: 68.9%), well below the 75% optimal threshold. Administrative and non-billable load consumes the remaining fee-earner capacity.
- Cited for: the billable-utilization figure.
- Used in: citations modal, section 2.
- URL: https://spiresearch.com/reports/2025-ps-maturity-benchmark/

## 3. Realization rate (unchanged)

- Finding: businesses typically collect around 85% of recorded work; the calculator applies a conservative 75% realization rate.
- Framed as generic "industry benchmarks"; no specific source asserted.

## Corrections log

- **Removed** the "up to 20% of their **billable week** hunting for information" framing. The cited source (McKinsey) states a share of the **workweek**, for _interaction workers_, not a billable share for this audience.
- **Removed** the "**1.8 hours every day**" figure — not stated by the cited source.
- **Removed** the attribution of "20% of billable capacity" to SPI Research. SPI publishes utilization percentages, not a "20% of billable capacity lost" headline; the claim is now stated as SPI's actual finding (66.4% utilization, 75% optimal).
